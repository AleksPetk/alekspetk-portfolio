About

I like building things from the ground up.

I’m a software developer focused on backend and full-stack development. What I enjoy most is taking an idea from nothing, defining how it should work, and turning it into a real product across backend, frontend, mobile, infrastructure, and deployment.

I prefer to understand the problem before rushing into code. I document the requirements, define the structure, think through the edge cases, and then build. I follow the same idea as the old saying: if I had six hours to cut down a tree, I’d spend four sharpening the axe.

I’m currently looking for a programming role where I can contribute, work with experienced engineers, and keep building real-world development experience.
